If you've brought a rack, Las Vegas is worth a visit. It mixes bolted lines (such as the classic *Welcome to Las Vegas*, 6b) with crack climbs where you place your own gear. Trad information for Tenerife is often old or hard to find, so check theCrag and ask at the local shop.

New to placing gear? A [private guiding day](/climbing-tenerife/private-guiding/) or a [course](/climbing-tenerife/courses/) is the safe way to start trad climbing on volcanic rock.
