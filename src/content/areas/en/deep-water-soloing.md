DWS means climbing above deep water without a rope, so falls end in the sea. The Atlantic is powerful, so read the sea before anything else. Waves under about **0.5 m** are good. With waves over **1 m**, DWS isn't possible.

## Safety

- Check the swell forecast and the tide, and look at the water before committing.
- Never go alone. Have a plan for getting out of the water, and keep a spotter on the rocks.
- Check the depth and look for submerged rocks before every line.
