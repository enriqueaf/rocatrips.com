Climbing in Las Cañadas, the giant crater beneath Mount Teide, is a completely different experience from the coastal gorges. The air is thin and dry, the light is intense, and the views across the lava fields to Teide are unforgettable. The area was re-bolted in recent years.

## Rules (Teide National Park)

- **Reservation required.** Book through [Tenerife ON](https://www.tenerifeon.es/en/routes/climbing/canada-del-el-capricho-climbing-area) before you go. Permits are strictly individual, and there are at most 58 places per day.
- **No new routes or bolting** inside the National Park.
- **Closures.** Sectors can close to protect birds and during the annual mouflon control. Check current notices before going.
- Stay on paths, take everything home, and don't build cairns or move rocks.

## Conditions

At high altitude the sun is strong even when the air is cool. Use high-SPF sun cream, bring warm layers for belaying, and expect cold mornings. It's the perfect summer crag when the south coast is too hot.
