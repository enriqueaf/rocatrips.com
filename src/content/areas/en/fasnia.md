Fasnia is a good escape when Arico is busy. It is included in the current *Escalada en Tenerife* guidebook and on theCrag and TheTopo, which have up-to-date topos.

Want a quieter crag day with someone who knows the way in? Book a [private guiding day](/climbing-tenerife/private-guiding/).
