## Crash pads and topos

You don't need to fly with a pad. The Roxtar shop in Villa de Arico rents crash pads by the day or week (see [shops and gear rental](/plan-your-trip/climbing-shops-and-gear-rental/)). Free topo PDFs for Arico Nuevo and Arico Ortiz are linked from Roxtar's climbing info page, and TheTopo has digital topos for every area.

## Combine with roped climbing

Many climbers spend half their trip bouldering and half on ropes. Book a [guided sport climbing day](/climbing-tenerife/guided-sport-climbing/) to get the best of both.
