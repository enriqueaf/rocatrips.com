Guaria is Tenerife's most impressive crag: a big, sunny wall of compact rock with long pitches, multi-pitch lines and serious trad. It isn't a beginner venue. Expect sustained climbing from about 6b, and bring a long rope.

## Permit: how to climb legally in Guaria

The Risco de Guaria lies within the **Montaña de Tejina** protected natural area, and climbing is allowed with a reservation:

1. Book a free permit at [centralreservas.tenerife.es](https://centralreservas.tenerife.es/?a=4). It is valid only for the date you choose.
2. There are at most **70 places per day**, bookable up to **90 days ahead**. Book early in high season.
3. Carry the permit and follow the protected-area rules it explains. Some routes may be closed for nesting birds.

## Conditions

Guaria faces the sun and gets hot. From October to April, aim for mornings or late afternoons. In summer it is usually too hot.
