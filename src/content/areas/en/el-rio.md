El Río is a long gorge with several distinct sectors, including *Presa Abajo*, *Fondo*, *Galería* and *El Acebuche*. Between them you'll find a big spread of grades and styles, which makes it a good choice for a full day when a group has mixed abilities.

## Conditions

Like the rest of the south, El Río is sunny and dry most of the year. In warm months choose a sector that's in the shade at the time of day you're climbing, and start early.
