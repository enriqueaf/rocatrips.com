## What it feels like

A holiday climbing day the way it should be. There's no circling for parking, no squinting at an old topo, and no warm-up wall that turns out to be in full sun. You walk in, the rope is flaked out, and the first route is waiting. The rock is unlike anything at home: sharp edges, pockets and long sustained walls that reward precise feet. By the last route you're climbing it like a local.

## How the day works

1. **Before the day.** We ask about your grade, experience and goals, then plan the crag around the forecast: shade when it's hot, shelter when it's windy.
2. **On the day.** We meet near the crag and walk in. Your guide sets the pace and picks routes that build through the day.
3. **Coaching.** Volcanic rock climbs differently from limestone or plastic. We show you how to use pockets, edges and friction, and how to rest on long sustained routes.
4. **Beta for later.** At the end of the day you get a list of crags and routes for the rest of your trip.

## Who it's for

Climbers comfortable on grade 5+/6a who want to make the most of limited holiday time in Tenerife, or who want a local's guidance before climbing independently.

Most Tenerife topos use French grades. See our [grade conversion table](/plan-your-trip/grades-and-levels/) for UIAA, UK and YDS equivalents.
