## What it feels like

Courses are about the moment you realise you don't need us any more. That might be clipping your first lead bolt, building your first anchor, or lowering off on your own and seeing your partner's thumbs-up from the ground. It's hands-on, unhurried learning on real rock, with a guide who has taught these skills many times and checks every step until it's second nature.

## Indoor to outdoor

The step from the gym to real rock. You'll learn reading routes on rock, falling and belaying safely outdoors, crag etiquette, and how to check fixed gear and lower-offs.

## Learn to lead

Lead climbing on bolts, step by step: clipping technique, rope management, safe falling, belaying a leader with a dynamic catch, and threading and lowering off at the top. Most people leave able to lead safely at a grade below their top-rope limit.

## Safety skills: anchors, lowering off and abseiling

The skills that make you independent: building and checking anchors, cleaning routes, abseiling with a backup, and simple problem-solving on the crag.

## Why learn in Tenerife?

The bolting is good, the routes are short, the approaches are easy and the weather is reliable all year. It's the perfect classroom, especially in winter when crags at home are cold or wet.
