## What it feels like

Courses are about the moment you realise you don't need us any more. That might be clipping your first lead bolt, taking your first deliberate fall, or seeing your partner's thumbs-up from the ground after a route you once thought was out of reach. It's hands-on, unhurried learning on real rock, with a guide who has taught these skills many times and checks every step until it's second nature.

They suit you whether you're taking your first steps towards becoming an independent climber or you're an experienced climber looking for specific, more advanced training to sharpen your skills.

## Indoor to outdoor

The step from the gym to real rock. You'll learn reading routes on rock, falling and belaying safely outdoors, crag etiquette, and how to check fixed gear and lower-offs.

## Learn to lead

Lead climbing on bolts, step by step: clipping technique, rope management, safe falling, belaying a leader with a dynamic catch, and threading and lowering off at the top.

## Fall training

Learn to fall safely and with confidence, building up gradually, so that fear of falling stops holding back your climbing.

## The mental game

Managing fear, committing to moves and staying calm on lead: the head side of climbing, worked on real routes.

## Why learn in Tenerife?

The basalt gives us an ideal playground, and it's wonderfully varied: from slabs and cracks to overhangs. The bolting is good, the routes are short, the approaches are easy and the weather is reliable all year. It's the perfect classroom, especially in winter when crags at home are cold or wet.
