## What you'll do

1. **Meet your guide** at the meeting point near the crag. We check your shoe size and fit your harness and helmet.
2. **Safety briefing.** You learn how the rope system works, the figure-of-eight knot, and the climbing calls.
3. **First climbs.** You climb easy, well-bolted routes on top-rope while your guide belays and coaches you on footwork.
4. **Learn to belay** (optional). If you'd like to, you learn to belay a partner under the guide's supervision.
5. **Harder routes.** As your confidence grows, you try a steeper or longer route.
6. **Debrief.** We give you tips on where to climb next, plus indoor gyms and courses if you're hooked.

## Who it's for

- Complete beginners who want to try climbing on real rock
- Indoor climbers who have never climbed outside
- Couples, friends and travellers looking for an active half day in Tenerife

## Where we climb

We usually use the easy, well-bolted sectors around [Tamadaya](/climbing-areas/tamadaya/) and [Arico](/climbing-areas/arico/), in the sunny south of the island. In summer we start early to avoid the heat.
