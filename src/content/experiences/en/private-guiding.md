## What it feels like

Just you, your people and a guide who's paying attention to nobody else. The day bends around you: an extra route when you're flying, a long lunch in the shade when it's hot, a harder line when you're ready to try it. It's the most personal way to climb in Tenerife.

## Ideas for a private day or week

- **Climbing week in Tenerife.** A mix of sport crags, a multi-pitch day in Guaria and a high-altitude day in Teide National Park.
- **Performance coaching.** Work a project, improve your footwork on volcanic rock, or train for redpoints.
- **Trad introduction.** Learn to place gear on the crack lines of Las Vegas.
- **Family or friends day.** Easy routes, games and photos, with nobody else in the group.
- **Photo day.** Climbing shots in spectacular volcanic scenery.

## How it works

Send us your dates, group size, experience and goals. We reply with a plan and a price, and once you're happy you book through our online booking page.
