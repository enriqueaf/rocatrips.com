## The strategy

1. **Start at sunrise** and finish by late morning on the coastal crags.
2. **Follow the shade.** Gorges have walls facing every direction, so pick the side out of the sun.
3. **Go high.** Cañada del Capricho in Teide National Park, at around 2,000 m, is much cooler than the coast (reservation required).
4. **Get wet.** Deep water soloing season runs from May to August, but only in calm seas.

## Heat safety

- Drink at least 2–3 L of water and take electrolytes.
- Wear a hat, sun hoodie and SPF 50.
- Watch for **calima** (hot Saharan air). On those days, cut the session short or rest.
- Volcanic rock gets very hot in direct sun, so it's hard on skin and on ropes left in the sun.

## Families on summer holiday

Summer is when families visit. Our [family climbing sessions](/climbing-tenerife/families-kids/) and [beginner days](/climbing-tenerife/beginners/) run in the early morning, in the shade, and finish before the heat.
