## Why Tenerife in winter

- **Warm, dry rock.** The south-coast crags are at their best, with mild days and low humidity.
- **Short flights** from most European cities, and Tenerife South airport is close to the crags.
- **Everything is open.** Climbers' hostels, the Roxtar shop and guided days all run through the season.

## Best crags for winter

Sunny walls in **Arico**, **El Río**, **Tamadaya** and **Fasnia**, and **Guaria** on cooler days (permit needed). Avoid **Teide National Park** in mid-winter, when it can be icy and freezing at altitude.

## Typical winter day

Start around 9:30 once the sun warms the rock, climb until sunset (around 18:30 in December), and bring a fleece for belaying in the shade.

## What to watch out for

- **Short showers** are possible from November to February, but the rock dries fast.
- **Trade-wind days** can feel cold on exposed crags, so choose a sheltered gorge.
- **Peak demand at Christmas, New Year and Easter**: book accommodation, hire cars, permits and guided days early.

## Make the most of your winter trip

A guided first day helps you find the right sectors fast, then you can climb independently for the rest of the week. See [guided sport climbing](/climbing-tenerife/guided-sport-climbing/) or plan a whole week with [private guiding](/climbing-tenerife/private-guiding/).
