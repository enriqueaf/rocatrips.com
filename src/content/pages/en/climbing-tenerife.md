Choose by your experience. **Beginner** and **family** sessions need no experience at all. **Courses** turn indoor climbers into independent outdoor climbers. **Guided sport climbing** and **multi-pitch** are for people who already climb. Not sure? Check [grades and levels](/plan-your-trip/grades-and-levels/) or [ask us](/contact/).

All experiences include technical equipment and a local guide who picks the best crag for your level and that day's conditions.
