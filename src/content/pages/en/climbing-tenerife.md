## What climbing with us feels like

The day starts quietly. You meet your guide at the edge of a dry volcanic gorge, with the rock still cool in the morning shade and the Atlantic flat and blue below. Harnesses go on and helmets click shut. Before anyone climbs, your guide walks you through the system: how the rope is anchored, what each call means, and why every knot gets checked twice.

Then you step onto the rock. Volcanic stone is rough and grippy, full of pockets and edges you couldn't see from the ground. Halfway up, most people stop and wonder what they've got themselves into. That's normal. Your guide is right there, calm, pointing out your next foothold. A few moves later you're at the top with the trade wind on your face and the whole south of Tenerife below you.

By the end of the morning the nerves have turned into something else. You're reading the rock, trusting your feet and asking for a harder route. That change, from "I can't" to "one more", is the part of the job we love most.

If you already climb, the feeling is different but just as good. You don't spend the day hunting for topos and parking or guessing which wall is in the sun. You climb the right routes, on the right rock, with someone who knows every line.

## Why it matters who you climb with

Climbing is safe when it's done well, and almost all of that depends on the guide. On a good day nobody notices the work: the anchor built with a backup, the loose block avoided, the crag swapped because the wind turned.

Tenerife has its own challenges. Volcanic rock can be loose and sharp away from the main lines, and the sun and heat are strong even in winter. Protected areas need permits, and some crags close seasonally for nesting birds. A guide who knows the island handles all of that for you, so you can just climb.

### Questions to ask any climbing guide in Tenerife

Whoever you book with, these questions tell you a lot:

1. **Are your guides qualified climbing guides?** In Spain, look for the official Técnico Deportivo en Escalada qualification, or an IFMGA/UIAGM mountain guide.
2. **Is the company registered for active tourism** with the Government of the Canary Islands?
3. **Does the price include liability and accident insurance?**
4. **How many climbers are there per guide?**
5. **How do you choose the crag, and what happens if the weather turns?**
6. **Do you handle permits** for protected areas such as Guaria?

You'll find our answers in [safety and our guides](/plan-your-trip/safety-and-guides/). If anything is unclear, [ask us](/contact/) before you book.
