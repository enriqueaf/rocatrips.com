## Which airport?

Fly into **Tenerife South (TFS)**. It's the closest airport to the main climbing area, about half an hour's drive from Villa de Arico on the TF-1 motorway. Tenerife North (TFN) works too, but adds roughly an hour of driving to the southern crags.

## Do I need a car?

For independent climbing, **yes**. Public transport doesn't reach the crags, and most areas are spread along the south-east coast and inland gorges. Book a hire car in advance for winter, when demand peaks.

## From the resorts

| From | To Arico area (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Costa Adeje / Playa de las Américas | 40–50 min |
| Los Cristianos | 40 min |
| El Médano | 25 min |
| Santa Cruz / La Laguna | 40–50 min |

For Guaria (Guía de Isora), Costa Adeje is the closest resort. For Teide National Park, allow at least an hour from the coast on winding roads.

## Parking etiquette

- Use obvious parking spots, and never block farm tracks, gates or driveways.
- Leave nothing visible in the car.
- Keep noise down near houses, and leave crags cleaner than you found them.
