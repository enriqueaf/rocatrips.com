Most visitors to Tenerife stay in the south-west resorts: Costa Adeje, Playa de las Américas and Los Cristianos. The good news is that real rock climbing is an easy half-day trip away.

## Where to climb from the resorts

- **Arico and the southern gorges** (40–50 min): the island's main climbing area, with hundreds of routes for every level. See [Arico](/climbing-areas/arico/) and [Tamadaya](/climbing-areas/tamadaya/).
- **Guaria, Guía de Isora** (closer to Adeje): a big wall for experienced climbers, with a permit required. See [Guaria](/climbing-areas/guaria/).
- **Teide National Park** (about 1 h): high-altitude climbing in a volcanic crater, with a reservation required. See [Cañada del Capricho](/climbing-areas/canada-del-capricho/).

## Getting there

Public transport doesn't reach the crags. With a hire car it's a straightforward drive along the TF-1. No car? Tell us where you're staying when you book, and we'll help you find the easiest way to join.
