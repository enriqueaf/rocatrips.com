## On a guided day with us

**We provide:** harness, helmet, ropes, quickdraws and all safety equipment, plus climbing shoes on beginner and family sessions.

**You bring:**
- Water: at least 1.5 L per person, or 2 L in warm months
- Snacks, or lunch on a full day
- Sun hat, SPF 50 sunscreen, sunglasses and lip balm
- Comfortable sportswear you can lift your knees in
- A light windproof or fleece for belaying
- Trainers or approach shoes for the walk-in
- A small backpack

## For an independent climbing trip

- **Rope:** 60 m minimum. A **70 m rope** is better for longer routes (Arico Abajo, Guaria)
- **Quickdraws:** 12–14
- Belay device, screwgate carabiners, a sling and a personal anchor for lowering off
- Helmet: rockfall happens on volcanic rock
- Small rack of cams and nuts if you plan trad (Las Vegas, Guaria)
- Crash pad for bouldering, or rent one locally (see [shops and gear rental](/plan-your-trip/climbing-shops-and-gear-rental/))
- Guidebook or app (see [topos and guidebooks](/plan-your-trip/topos-and-guidebooks/))

## Clothing for Tenerife's climate

The sun is strong all year, and the wind can be cool. Pack light, breathable layers, a sun hoodie, and a warm layer for winter mornings. For Teide National Park, bring a proper warm jacket, hat and gloves in winter.

## Flying with climbing gear

Ropes, harnesses and quickdraws go in checked luggage. Want to travel light? Book a [guided day](/climbing-tenerife/) and use our equipment, or rent locally.
