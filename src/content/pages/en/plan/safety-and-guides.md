Climbing is an adventure sport with real risks. We manage those risks the way professional mountain guides do in the Alps: clear procedures, the right equipment, small groups, and conservative decisions.

## Our standards

- **Qualified, experienced guides.** Our lead guide, Pablo Vicens, is a Técnico Deportivo Superior en Escalada (TD3), the highest level of Spain's official climbing-guide qualification. He has 12 years of professional guiding experience and knows each crag's conditions, bolting and loose sections.
- **Redundant systems.** Every anchor has a backup, and beginners climb on top-rope, so the rope is always anchored above them.
- **Two climbers per guide, maximum**, so every belay and knot is checked by the guide.
- **Equipment** is inspected regularly and retired on schedule. Helmets are mandatory.
- **Partner checks** (harness, knot, belay device, end of rope knotted) on every climb, a habit we teach you for life.
- **Weather and conditions**: we choose crags by sun, wind and heat, and we cancel or change plans when it isn't safe.
- **First aid kit and communication** on every outing.

- **Guide qualifications.** Pablo Vicens holds Spain's Técnico Deportivo Superior en Escalada (TD3), the highest level of the official climbing-guide qualification, and a current first-aid certificate. He has guided professionally for 12 years in Tenerife and mainland Spain.

- **Choosing the crag.** We choose it for your level, then for sun, wind and crowds on the day. If conditions are unsafe, we move to another crag, reschedule or refund you (see our [cancellation policy](/legal/cancellation/)).
- **Permits.** We organise the free permit for Guaria and any other protected area we climb in.

## Volcanic rock: what's different

Tenerife's rock is sound on established routes, but volcanic rock can have loose blocks and sharp edges, especially away from the main lines. Wear a helmet, don't belay directly under the climber, test holds you're unsure of, and protect the rope from sharp edges.

## Insurance

We recommend travel insurance that covers rock climbing and mountain rescue. Members of alpine clubs, such as the Austrian Alpenverein (ÖAV), the German Alpenverein (DAV) or the British Mountaineering Council (BMC), should check what their membership covers abroad.

## Emergencies

The European emergency number **112** works throughout Tenerife.
