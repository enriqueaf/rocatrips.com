Tenerife's outdoor climbing is so good that most visitors never go indoors, but gyms are handy on a rare rainy day, for training, or for locals in the north.

| Gym | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rocódromo El Muro | Avenida Los Menceyes 113, La Laguna | Bouldering walls (about 3.8 m) with 5,000+ holds, a campus area and a small fitness zone. Gear for rent or sale |
| Cave Rock | Calle Tamarco 13, La Laguna | About 250 m² of walls with 3,000+ holds, including overhangs and roofs |
| Rocódromo Municipal de Ofra | Santa Cruz de Tenerife | Reported to be for federated members only, so check access |

There are no major climbing gyms near the southern resorts. The nearest real climbing from Costa Adeje is outdoors. Try a [beginner day](/climbing-tenerife/beginners/).

*Check opening hours and prices directly with each gym.*
