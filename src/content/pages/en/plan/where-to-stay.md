For a climbing-focused trip, stay in or near **Villa de Arico**, a few minutes from the main crags. For a family holiday with some climbing, the south-coast resorts work well with a car.

## Climbers' accommodation in Arico

| Place | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Tenerife Climbing House](https://tenerifeclimbinghouse.com/) | Climbers' hostel | In Villa de Arico, practically a rope length from the crags. Rooms, a garden and a climbers' atmosphere, in the same building as the Roxtar shop |
| [Tenerife Lodge](https://tenerifelodge.com/) | Climbing accommodation | Accommodation in the south of Tenerife aimed at climbers |
| Basecamp Ortiz | Community hostel | Hostel-style climbers' base in the Arico area |

Holiday apartments in Villa de Arico, Arico Nuevo and Poris de Abona are also popular. Look for places with a kitchen and parking.

## Other bases

| Base | Good for | Drive to Arico |
|---|---|---|
| El Médano | Windsurf and beach vibe, close to TFS airport | ~25 min |
| Los Cristianos | Ferries, restaurants, families | ~40 min |
| Costa Adeje / Playa de las Américas | Hotels, families, nightlife; close to Guaria | ~40–50 min |
| La Laguna | City life, climbing gyms, the north | ~45 min |

## Tips

- In winter (peak season), book early, especially climbers' hostels.
- A car is essential if you're not staying in Arico.
- On a family holiday in Costa Adeje? A [family climbing session](/climbing-tenerife/families-kids/) or [beginner day](/climbing-tenerife/beginners/) is an easy way to add climbing.
