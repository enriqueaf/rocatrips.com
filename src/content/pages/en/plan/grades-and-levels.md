faq:
  - q: What grading system is used in Tenerife?
    a: Tenerife sport routes use the French grading system (4, 5a, 6a, 6b+, 7a, …). Bouldering uses Fontainebleau grades (6A, 7A, …).
  - q: What is 6a in UIAA grades?
    a: French 6a is roughly UIAA VI to VI+, the grades used in Austria and Germany.
  - q: What grade do I need to climb in Tenerife?
    a: None for a guided beginner day. To climb independently, being able to lead about 5c–6a gives you plenty of choice in Arico and the southern gorges.

Tenerife topos use **French sport grades**. If you're used to the **UIAA** scale from Austria, Germany or Switzerland, or to UK or US grades, use the table below. Conversions are approximate. Volcanic rock and local styles vary, so treat the first routes as a calibration.

## Grade conversion table

| French | UIAA (AT/DE/CH) | UK tech | USA (YDS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | IV | 4a | 5.5–5.6 |
| 4+ | IV+/V- | 4b | 5.7 |
| 5a | V | 4c | 5.8 |
| 5b | V+ | 4c/5a | 5.9 |
| 5c | VI- | 5a | 5.10a |
| 6a | VI | 5b | 5.10a/b |
| 6a+ | VI+ | 5b/5c | 5.10b |
| 6b | VII- | 5c | 5.10c |
| 6b+ | VII | 5c/6a | 5.10d |
| 6c | VII+ | 6a | 5.11a/b |
| 6c+ | VIII- | 6a | 5.11c |
| 7a | VIII | 6a/6b | 5.11d |
| 7a+ | VIII+ | 6b | 5.12a |
| 7b | VIII+/IX- | 6b | 5.12b |
| 7b+ | IX- | 6b/6c | 5.12c |
| 7c | IX | 6c | 5.12d |
| 7c+ | IX+ | 6c | 5.13a |
| 8a | IX+/X- | 6c/7a | 5.13b |
