## Roxtar (Villa de Arico)

The island's main climbing shop, right in the climbing village of Arico and in the same building as Tenerife Climbing House.

- **Address:** Calle La Asomadita 8, 38580 Villa de Arico
- **What you'll find:** shoes, harnesses, ropes, cams, chalk, clothing, guidebooks and maps, plus a small boulder wall for testing shoes, and coffee
- **Rental:** crash pads, clip sticks, climbing shoes, harnesses, quickdraws and belay devices (ropes under special conditions), plus baby carriers and trekking poles
- **Guidebook:** the current *Escalada en Tenerife* (Spanish and English)
- **Hours:** Monday–Saturday, morning and afternoon. The shop usually closes in mid-summer, so check before you go
- **Website:** [roxtar.es](https://www.roxtar.es/)

Roxtar also supports the bolting and maintenance of Tenerife's sport routes, so buying the local guidebook helps keep the routes safe.

## Tenerife Outdoor (La Laguna)

A mountain shop in the north of the island for hiking, trekking, canyoning and mountaineering gear.

- **Address:** Avenida de la República Argentina 79, San Cristóbal de La Laguna
- **Website:** [tenerifeoutdoor.com](https://www.tenerifeoutdoor.com/)

## Tips

- **In winter, reserve rental gear by email**, because crash pads and shoes go fast in peak season.
- Bring your own climbing shoes if you can. Rental sizes are limited.
- Don't want to rent or carry gear? On our [guided days](/climbing-tenerife/) all technical equipment is included.

*Details change, so check opening hours and rental conditions with each shop before your visit.*
