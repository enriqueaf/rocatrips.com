## Printed guidebooks

| Guidebook | Coverage | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| *Escalada en Tenerife* (Spanish/English) | Almost 1,300 routes in 17 sport crags, mostly in the south | Roxtar in Villa de Arico, and climbing retailers |
| *Tenerife Sport Climbing* (Ediciones Desnivel, 2nd ed. 2023) | About 1,300 sport routes | UK and European climbing shops |

The 17 crags in the current guidebook are Fasnia, La Galería, Tamadaya, Planeta Zarza, Arico, Los Naranjos, La Fuente, Fantasma, El Hoyo, El Río, Zona Zero, Las Barbas de San Pedro, Las Vegas, Las Palomas, La Martela, Guaria and El Capricho.

Part of the profits from the local guidebooks goes to re-bolting, so buying one directly helps keep the routes safe.

## Online topos and apps

- **[theCrag: Tenerife](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/spain/tenerife)**. Community database with route lists, grades, access notes and logbooks. Start with [South Tenerife](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/spain/tenerife/area/629583144) or [Arico Arriba](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/spain/tenerife/arico).
- **[TheTopo (formerly 27crags): Tenerife](https://thetopo.com/areas/tenerife)**. 2,000+ routes in 29 crags, with photo topos for sport, trad, bouldering and deep water soloing (premium).

## Free bouldering guides

Roxtar links free PDF guides to the **Arico Nuevo** and **Arico Ortiz** boulder areas from its [climbing info page](https://www.roxtar.es/climb%20info.htm). See also our [bouldering guide](/climbing-areas/bouldering-tenerife/).

## Trad and multi-pitch

Information on trad and multi-pitch routes is often old or hard to find. If you're not sure about a line, build your own belays, carry a rack, or climb it with a guide. See [guided multi-pitch](/climbing-tenerife/multi-pitch/).

Rather skip the topo hunting? On a [guided day](/climbing-tenerife/guided-sport-climbing/) we choose the routes for you.
