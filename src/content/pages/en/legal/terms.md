## 1. Booking

Bookings are made online through our booking partner. A booking is confirmed when you receive a confirmation email. By booking, you accept these terms and our [cancellation policy](/legal/cancellation/).

## 2. Prices and payment

Prices are per person unless stated otherwise and include the services listed on each experience page. Payment is made when booking through our booking partner.

## 3. Participant responsibilities

- Give honest information about your experience, fitness and health.
- Follow the guide's instructions at all times, including on safety equipment.
- Minors must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian unless agreed otherwise in writing.
- Don't take part under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

## 4. Risk

Rock climbing is an outdoor adventure activity with inherent risks, including falls, falling rock and changing weather. We reduce these risks with professional procedures and equipment, but they can't be removed completely. By taking part you accept these inherent risks.

## 5. Changes by Rocatrips

We may change the crag, route or programme for safety, weather, access or group-level reasons. See the [cancellation policy](/legal/cancellation/) for weather cancellations.

## 6. Photos

Guides may take photos during activities. We'll only publish photos in which you're recognisable with your consent.

## 7. Applicable law

These terms are governed by Spanish law.
