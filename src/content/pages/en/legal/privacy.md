## Who is responsible

Rocatrips is responsible for the personal data processed through this website. Contact: [hello@rocatrips.com](mailto:hello@rocatrips.com).

## What data we collect

- **When you contact us:** your name, email, and the information you include in your message.
- **When you book:** the data you give our booking partner (name, contact details, number of participants, and relevant information such as experience level or health notes).
- **When you visit the website:** we use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics that don't identify you personally.

## Why we use it

To answer your questions, manage your booking, and keep you and the group safe during activities. The legal bases are your consent, and performance of the contract you enter by booking.

## Who we share it with

Our booking partner, which processes bookings on our behalf, and nobody else, except where the law requires it. We never sell your data.

## How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purpose above, or as required by tax and accounting law.

## Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection by emailing us. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es/)).
