## If you cancel

- **More than 48 hours before the activity:** free cancellation, full refund.
- **Less than 48 hours before:** no refund, but we'll try to move you to another date if availability allows.
- **No-show:** no refund.

## If we cancel (weather or safety)

Your safety comes first. If conditions are unsafe because of rain, strong wind, extreme heat, calima or closures, we will:

1. offer an alternative crag the same day if one suits your level, or
2. reschedule to another date, or
3. give you a **full refund**.

## Changes and late arrival

Want to change the date? Contact us as early as possible. If you arrive late we'll do our best, but the activity may need to finish at the planned time.

## Health and fitness

Tell us about any medical conditions, injuries or pregnancy when booking. The guide may stop or adapt the activity if a participant's safety, or anyone else's, is at risk. In that case the activity counts as completed.
