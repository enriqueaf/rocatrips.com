faq:
  - q: Can I go rock climbing in Tenerife with no experience?
    a: Yes. Our beginner climbing day is designed for complete beginners. A local guide teaches you everything and provides all the equipment.
  - q: When is the best time to climb in Tenerife?
    a: All year. October to May is the main season in the south. In summer we climb early, in the shade, or at altitude in Teide National Park.
  - q: Where is the best climbing in Tenerife?
    a: Arico, in the south-east, is the main area, with 260+ sport routes. El Río, Tamadaya and Fasnia are nearby, Guaria has long routes, and Cañada del Capricho is in Teide National Park.
  - q: Is equipment included?
    a: Yes. Harness, helmet, ropes and safety gear are included in every experience, and climbing shoes are included on beginner and family sessions.
