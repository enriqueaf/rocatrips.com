# Rocatrips: keyword research v0.1 (2026-11-17)

## Method and limits

Google Trends couldn't be read automatically: the explore page renders only in a browser, and repeated requests are rate-limited. So no Trends numbers are included here. Instead, keyword choices are based on **what already ranks and sells**:

- page titles and product names of the competitors and marketplaces that rank for Tenerife climbing (Ocho Escalada, Yumping, Manawa, Civitatis, Atrápalo, Picuco, GetYourGuide-type listings)
- the guide and database sites that dominate informational searches (theCrag, TheTopo, Roxtar, Desnivel, bergsteigen.com)
- German and Spanish wording used by those sites

**Next step (10 minutes in a browser):** run the Trends comparisons in section 4 and Google Search Console once the site is live, then adjust titles.

## 3. Audience notes

- **Austria, Germany, Switzerland:** UIAA grades, "Kletterkurs", "Klettergarten" and "Mehrseillängen" vocabulary, and expectations of clear *Voraussetzungen* (requirements), *Leistungen* (included services), *Treffpunkt* (meeting point) and *Stornobedingungen* (cancellation terms). These are now on every experience page. bergsteigen.com already covers Planeta Zarza and Cañada del Capricho, so it's a link-outreach target.
- **UK and Scandinavia:** "winter sun rock", British grades, UKClimbing articles. Outreach target: UKC destination listings.
- **Holidaymakers in Costa Adeje:** "things to do", "activities", "with kids". Marketplace listings dominate, so list Rocatrips on GetYourGuide or Civitatis as well as running the site.

## 4. Google Trends checks to run (browser)

Use `trends.google.com/trends/explore`, timeframe **past 5 years**, worldwide, then repeat for **Austria, Germany, UK and Spain**:

1. `climbing tenerife` vs `escalada tenerife` vs `klettern teneriffa`. Which language market is biggest?
2. `climbing tenerife` vs `climbing costa adeje` vs `climbing teide`
3. `climbing course tenerife` vs `climbing tenerife beginners` vs `rock climbing tenerife`
4. `kletterkurs teneriffa` vs `klettern teneriffa anfänger`
5. Look at **seasonality** (expect peaks October–March) and the **Related queries → Rising** list.

Update titles and H1s with the winning variants, and schedule the seasonal articles (winter and summer) to go live about 6 weeks before each peak.
